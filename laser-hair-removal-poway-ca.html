Laser Hair Removal FAQs — Poway patients ask

How many laser hair removal sessions will I need?

Most areas require 6-8 sessions spaced 4-6 weeks apart for optimal reduction. Hormonal areas (face, chin) sometimes benefit from a few extra. Maintenance is typically 1-2 sessions per year.

Does laser hair removal hurt?

Most patients describe it as a quick rubber-band snap. Our platform has built-in cooling and we offer numbing cream for sensitive areas like the bikini or face.

Can I shave between sessions?

Yes — shaving between treatments is encouraged. Do not wax, pluck, or use depilatory creams; the laser needs the hair root intact in the follicle.
