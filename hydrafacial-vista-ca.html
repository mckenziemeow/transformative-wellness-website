HydraFacial FAQs — Vista patients ask

How often should I get a HydraFacial?

Monthly is the sweet spot — it aligns with your skin's natural turnover cycle. Brides and event-goers often book a series of 3 in the weeks before.

HydraFacial vs. regular facial — what's the difference?

A standard facial is largely manual (steaming, hands-on extractions). HydraFacial uses a patented vortex tip that suctions debris while infusing serums — it's gentler, faster, and more consistent than manual extractions.

Can I get HydraFacial with sensitive skin or rosacea?

Yes — we customize boosters and pressure to skin type. Many rosacea patients tolerate HydraFacial well when stronger treatments aren't safe.
