FAQ

Membership Questions

How does the monthly credit work?

Your Beauty Bank service credit is automatically added to your account each month. Use it on any service: Botox, facials, laser, IV therapy, anything! Credits roll over and never expire while you're a member.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! Both memberships are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

How do member savings work?

Members get exclusive pricing on injectables and a monthly discount on medical weight-loss, on both tiers. Beauty Bank adds a monthly service credit and member-only pricing on packages. À-la-carte services have one honest price: your membership saves you through credit and member rates, not a blanket percentage.

Can I switch memberships?

Absolutely! You can switch between Beauty Bank and Perks at any time. Your unused credits will remain on your account.

Is there a sign-up fee?

No! There are no sign-up fees, annual fees, or hidden costs. Just your monthly membership fee.

Can family members use my membership?

Membership benefits are for the primary member only. However, we offer family discounts: ask us about adding family members!
