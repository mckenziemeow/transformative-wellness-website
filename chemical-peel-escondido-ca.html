Chemical Peel FAQs — Escondido patients ask

Which chemical peel is right for me?

Lighter peels (lactic, salicylic) are good monthly maintenance with no downtime. Medium peels (TCA, Jessner) deliver stronger results but require 3-7 days of healing. We choose based on your skin type and how much downtime you can take.

How often can I get a chemical peel?

Light peels can be done every 2-4 weeks. Medium peels are typically spaced 6-12 weeks apart. We build series tailored to your goals.

Will my skin peel visibly?

Light peels usually cause subtle flaking by day 2-3. Medium peels produce visible peeling for 3-7 days — you can predict downtime based on the depth we choose.
